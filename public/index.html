Privacy Policy

We have developed Pixely: Relaxing Pixel Art as an Ad-supported/Freemium app. This service is provided by us at no cost and is intended for use as is.

We take the privacy of our users—especially children—very seriously. We are committed to complying with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play’s Families Policy.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

We designed this app to be safe for kids. We do not ask for any personal information.

If you are a parent or guardian and you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at info.tenhead@gmail.com so that we will be able to remove such information.

To improve the app's stability and fix bugs, we may collect non-personal, anonymous technical data using third-party services. This data cannot be used to identify a specific individual. This includes:

  • Device type (e.g., Tablet or Phone)
  • Operating system version (e.g., Android 12)
  • Crash logs (if the app closes unexpectedly)

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

We may use third-party services that collect information used to identify your device for non-behavioral purposes (such as frequency capping or debugging).

If our app displays advertisements, we only use Certified Ad Networks suitable for families (e.g., AdMob for Families). We ensure that:

  • Ads are appropriate for children and general audiences.
  • No behavioral tracking is used (we do not track what other websites your child visits).
  • Ads are "Contextual" only (based on the current content of the app).

Links to privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

4. Device Permissions

Our app may request the following permissions to function correctly:

5. In-App Purchases & Parental Gates

Our app contains optional In-App Purchases (e.g., to unlock premium drawings or remove ads).

All purchases are protected by a Parental Gate (a mechanism requiring an adult action, such as a math problem or a specific gesture) to prevent accidental purchases by children.

Transactions are handled entirely by the Google Play Store. We do not process or store credit card information.
