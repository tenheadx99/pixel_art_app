Privacy Policy

App: 3 Tiles Match: Animal Puzzle Game (Package: com.tenhead.tilematch)
Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 3 Tiles Match: Animal Puzzle Game ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application. We provide this service as an Ad-supported game intended for use as is.

We respect the privacy of all our users—especially children and families. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play's Families Policy and Developer Distribution Agreement.

1. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy Compliance)

Our app is designed to be entertaining and safe for all ages, including children and general audiences.

  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, physical addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise GPS location from children under the age of 13.
  • No account registration or social login is required to play 3 Tiles Match.
  • If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has somehow provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at info.tenhead@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete the data from our records.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Game Progress & Local Storage:
All gameplay data—including completed levels, high scores, earned coins, sound preferences, unlocked themes, and lucky wheel timers—is stored locally on your device via local storage (SharedPreferences). This data stays entirely on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Non-Personal Technical Data:
To ensure app stability, fix crashes, and optimize puzzle performance across various mobile devices, third-party software development kits (SDKs) may collect anonymous, non-identifying technical data. This may include:

  • Device hardware model and manufacturer
  • Operating system version (e.g., Android 13)
  • Crash reports and anonymous app performance diagnostics

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep 3 Tiles Match free to play, the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads for extra in-game revives/coins).

We strictly configure advertising to adhere to Google Play’s Families Policy and child privacy regulations:

  • Appropriate Ad Content: Only ads rated suitable for general audiences and families are requested and displayed.
  • No Behavioral / Interest-Based Tracking: Ads are non-personalized and contextual. We do not track user activities across other apps or websites.
  • Google Certified Ad Networks: We only integrate certified ad networks verified for compliance with Google Play's Families requirements.

Third-party privacy policies for services used in the app:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob Privacy & COPPA

4. Device Permissions

3 Tiles Match operates with minimal permissions and does not require sensitive permissions:

  • Internet Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required solely to load contextual advertisements and check for app updates via the Google Play Store.
  • No Access to Photos, Contacts, or Microphone: We do not access your photos, camera, microphone, contact lists, or precise location.

5. In-App Features & Reset Progress

You have full control over your game data. At any time, you can reset all game progress, levels, and coins directly inside the game settings via the Reset Progress button.

6. Data Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting information. Since the app does not transmit or store personal data on remote servers, your local gameplay remains private on your own device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date. You are encouraged to review this page periodically for updates.
