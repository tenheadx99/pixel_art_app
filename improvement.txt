================================================================================
                    PIXEL ART APP & ADMIN - FEATURE SUGGESTIONS & ROADMAP
================================================================================

1. ENGAGEMENT & RETENTION (DAILY ACTIVE USERS & RETENTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Daily Art Calendar & History Archive:
  - Add a Monthly Calendar View where users can browse and see past completed 
    days with golden stamps.
  - Allow players to unlock and play missed past days using diamonds or rewarded 
    ads.
  - Protects streaks while creating a natural diamond sink and satisfying 
    completionist motivation.

* Thematic Collections & Album Badges (e.g., "Anime Legends", "Floral Garden"):
  - Group 5-8 related artworks into curated "Albums" or "Packs".
  - Completing all artworks in an album grants a unique collectible badge, 
    diamond rewards, and a special animated completion screen.
  - Dynamic Admin Support: Admins can curate, publish, and order albums from the 
    admin panel via Firestore without requiring app store updates.

* Time-Lapse Replay & Video Export:
  - Track coloring actions and replay the artwork from a blank grid to the 
    finished masterpiece in high-speed motion.
  - Enable 1-tap export as MP4 / GIF with app branding and watermarks, optimized 
    for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and WhatsApp Stories (driving organic viral 
    growth).


2. GAMEPLAY & COLORING USER EXPERIENCE (UX DELIGHT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Continuous Drag-to-Color (Long-Press Swipe Fill):
  - Enhance drag-to-fill with smooth haptic feedback ticks as consecutive 
    matching numbered cells are filled in a continuous swipe.
  - Highly satisfying tactile feel, especially on large 64x64 or 128x128 
    canvases.

* Flavor-Specific Visual Overlays & Shaders:
  - Custom texture overlays / shaders tailored for each app flavor:
    * Diamond: Subtle beveled gem reflections.
    * Stitch / CrossStitch: Realistic woven thread texture.
    * Glow: Vibrant neon bloom effects.
    * Mosaic: Grout line shading and glazed tile look.

* Offline Artwork Pack Downloads:
  - Allow users to download entire category packs or custom shelves for offline 
    coloring (ideal for flights, commutes, and low-connectivity environments).


3. CONTENT MANAGEMENT & LIVEOPS (ADMIN PANEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Visual Release Scheduler & Expiry Management:
  - In Admin panel, provide a calendar UI to schedule artwork releases 
    (e.g., "Release on Dec 25 at 00:00").
  - Show countdown badges in both Admin and Mobile app ("Releases in 2 days").

* A/B Testing Home Shelves & Layouts:
  - Support publishing multiple home shelf layouts (Variant A vs Variant B).
  - Track completion rates and engagement metrics per layout to optimize 
    onboarding and content discovery.

* Bulk Artwork Importer with AI Auto-Tagging:
  - Drag-and-drop batch upload for ZIP archives containing multiple pixel art 
    images.
  - Auto-calculates dimensions, color counts, and difficulty, and suggests tags 
    and categories automatically before batch publishing.


4. MONETIZATION & IN-GAME ECONOMY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 7-Day Daily Login Reward Wheel / Ladder:
  - Day 1: +10 Diamonds
  - Day 3: +1 Free Streak Freeze
  - Day 5: 2x Magic Wand Booster
  - Day 7: +100 Diamonds + Exclusive Mystery Artwork
  - Directly boosts D1 to D7 user retention rates.

* Rewarded Ad Reward Multipliers:
  - On completing an artwork, offer: "Claim 20 Diamonds" or "Watch Video to 3x 
    (60 Diamonds)".
  - Consistently one of the highest-converting, non-intrusive rewarded ad formats 
    in mobile puzzle games.

* Seasonal Battle Pass / "Artist Pass":
  - 30-day progression track: coloring cells and finishing artworks awards XP.
  - Free track and VIP track unlocking exclusive seasonal artwork packs, 
    special palette animations, and bonus power-ups.
